Architecture Breakdown

Private Inference

Run your models on your own hardware. The heavy lifting of inference stays entirely at home.

Direct Connection

FolliA talks directly to your machine over your local network. Away from home, use your VPN or an HTTPS address to encrypt the traffic.

Zero Proxies

No external cloud servers, no hidden telemetry. Your prompts and answers belong to you exclusively.

Getting Started

  1. 1

    Install Ollama

    On your computer or server, install Ollama, then download a model, for example ollama pull llama3.2.

  2. 2

    Open it to your local network

    By default, Ollama only accepts connections from the machine itself. Set the OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0 variable, restart Ollama and allow port 11434 in your firewall.

  3. 3

    Connect FolliA

    In Settings > Server, enter the machine's IP address and port 11434, pick your model and start chatting.

Away from home, go through your VPN rather than exposing Ollama's port to the Internet: it has no authentication.
